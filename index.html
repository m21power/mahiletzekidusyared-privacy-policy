Our promise

  • No account
  • No data collected
  • No ads
  • No tracking

§ 1About the App

ማሕሌት ዘቅዱስ ያሬድ lets you read the Wazema and Mahilet liturgical texts (ሥርዓተ ዋዜማ and ሥርዓተ ማኅሌት) for the feast days of each month, and lets you share them or save them as a PDF. The yearly content is included with the App for you to read.

§ 2Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask you to sign up, log in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal detail.

The App does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and we do not run a server or database that receives information about you or how you use the App.

§ 3Sharing & Saving as PDF

When you choose to share a text or save it as a PDF, this is done on your own device using your device's standard sharing and file features.

  • Saved PDFs are stored where you choose to save them.
  • When you share, the content goes to the app or person you select. We never see it and it is not sent to us.
  • What those other apps do with shared content is governed by their own privacy policies.

§ 4Advertising & Third Parties

The App contains no advertisements and does not share any data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties, because there is no data to share.

§ 5Permissions

The App does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or personal files.

§ 6Children's Privacy

Since the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. The App is suitable for readers of all ages.

§ 7Changes to This Policy

If the App's features change in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
